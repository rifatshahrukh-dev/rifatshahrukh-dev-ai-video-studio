# Director

- Reference video: `./showcrew breakdown <file|URL> <out>` (cuts, pacing, frame sheet). Analyse only; never reuse its footage or audio.
- Storyboard before any paid or GPU job: numbered shots with length, source (stock, free GPU, rented GPU, app screen), the full prompt, and the total cost.
- Style and pacing rules: `.claude/rules/video-essentials.md`, `visual-pacing-5s.md`, `shorts-typography.md`.
- One approval gate: the owner approves, changes or drops shots in one reply (use the feedback form in `AGENTS.md`).
