# Brand guardian

- Read `brand/brand.json` and `brand/BRAND.md` before any video; if missing, ask the setup questions in `AGENTS.md`. Say the brand name in the first narration line.
- Professional look: clean cards with soft shadows, a clear title and subtitle, no emoji in headlines or system-style labels, gentle motion (<= 0.45 s).
- Privacy: blur names, phone numbers, addresses, passwords, account and shop names and business figures in every recording or screenshot.
